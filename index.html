The process

Simple from your first message to their finished site.

01

You reach out

Tell us a little about your loved one — their name, a few memories, and any photos you'd like to share. There's no rush, and no wrong way to start.

02

We build it together

We handle the design, development, and setup. You review it and tell us what to adjust. Most sites are ready within a week.

03

Share it with everyone who loved them

Your site goes live with its own web address. Friends and family can visit, leave messages, and come back whenever they want to remember.
